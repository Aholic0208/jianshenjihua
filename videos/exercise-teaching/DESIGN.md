## Style Prompt

Create calm, trustworthy fitness teaching videos that feel like part of the existing training app: clean health-focused visuals, soft studio lighting, restrained motion, clear hierarchy, and practical coaching emphasis over flashy promo energy. The viewer should feel guided by a professional coach, not sold to by an ad.

## Colors

- `#f4f7f5` background wash
- `#ffffff` main surface
- `#16211d` primary text
- `#1c6b4b` coaching emphasis
- `#2563eb` secondary highlight
- `#b76e1f` caution accent

## Typography

- `"Segoe UI", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", sans-serif`
- Strong numeric and label contrast, no decorative display font

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use neon, club-like lighting, or gaming-style motion
- Do not fill the frame with gradient blobs or abstract decoration
- Do not use tiny labels or crowded charts that are hard to read in video
- Do not make the pacing feel like an advertisement
- Do not introduce colors that fight the app's green-blue instructional palette
